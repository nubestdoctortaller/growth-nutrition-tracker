Why these five habits

Protein at every meal
Protein supplies the building blocks for bone and muscle, and supports IGF-1, a hormone tied to bone growth. Try eggs, chicken, Greek yogurt, beans, or tofu.
Two or three calcium foods a day
Calcium is the mineral bones are built from, and needs rise from around age 9 through the teen years. Milk, cheese, yogurt, fortified plant milks, and sardines all count.
A vitamin C fruit or vegetable every day
Vitamin C helps the body build collagen and absorb iron from plant foods. Bell peppers, broccoli, oranges, and strawberries are easy picks.
Fish twice a week
Salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide vitamin D, which the body needs to absorb calcium, plus omega-3s linked to healthy bone mineralization.
Iron and zinc foods three times a week
Iron carries oxygen to growing tissue and zinc supports cell growth. Lean beef, lentils, chickpeas, and pumpkin seeds cover both.

Food works alongside the other basics of growth: enough sleep and daily physical activity.
